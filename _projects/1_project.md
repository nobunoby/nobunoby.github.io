**Task-Oriented Active Learning of Residual Dynamics for Model Predictive Path Integral Control**  
Nobuaki Aoki, Hojin Lee, Stefan Sosnowski, and Sandra Hirche (2026)

[Read the paper on arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.19378)

The work studies **task-oriented information acquisition (ToIA)** in model predictive path integral control (MPPI) with online Gaussian-process residual dynamics learning. Rather than exploring solely to reduce model uncertainty, the method scores possible learning opportunities by their estimated relevance to later states on sampled control rollouts and to the control task.

The approach is evaluated in simulated off-road navigation with heterogeneous terrain. See the [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.19378) for the formulation, experiments, and comparisons.
