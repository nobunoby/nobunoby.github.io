I am **Nobuaki Aoki**, a PhD researcher at the [Chair of Information-oriented Control (ITR)](https://www.ce.cit.tum.de/en/itr/) at the **Technical University of Munich (TUM)**. My research focuses on **learning-based control and decision-making under uncertainty** for autonomous robotic systems.

I am particularly interested in how learning and control can be designed together: learning dynamics models that are useful for downstream decisions, collecting information that matters for the task, and making planning and control robust to imperfect models and observations.

Before joining TUM in October 2025, I worked as a researcher at **Hitachi's Research and Development Group** in Japan. I received my M.Sc. (2023) and B.Eng. (2021) from **Keio University**, and I was an exchange student at TUM during 2019–2020.

### Research interests

- **Learning-based control:** Model predictive control, online adaptation, and robust control using learned dynamics.
- **Decision-aware model learning and active learning:** Learning and acquiring information based on its value to planning and control rather than prediction accuracy alone.
- **Planning and decision-making under uncertainty:** Risk-aware planning and control in uncertain, changing environments.
- **Robot autonomy:** Applications to field and mobile robots, including aerial and off-road systems.
