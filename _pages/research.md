I study reliable and adaptive robotic autonomy under uncertainty, combining learning-based control, probabilistic modeling, optimization, and robot learning. My established work on Gaussian Process models and sampling-based control provides a foundation for emerging investigations into decision-aware models and policy optimization.

## Research scope

Across these directions, I investigate how models can better support control decisions, how differentiable policy optimization can remain reliable under model mismatch, and how learned dynamics uncertainty can inform risk-aware planning. Another interest is navigation where uncertain terrain perception and uncertain physical dynamics jointly affect decisions.

## Research directions

### Uncertainty-Aware Learning-Based Control

My current research centers on **Gaussian Process (GP) residual dynamics learning** and **Model Predictive Path Integral Control (MPPI)**. I study uncertainty propagation, risk-aware planning, and task-oriented active dynamics learning, balancing exploration, control performance, and safety when robot dynamics are uncertain.

### Decision-Aware Model Learning and World Models

An emerging direction is to learn and evaluate dynamics models for downstream planning and control, rather than prediction error alone. My interests include value-aware and decision-aware learning, task-relevant information acquisition, and world models for robot decision-making, including active visual and physical exploration for manipulation under uncertainty.

### Robot Learning and Differentiable Policy Optimization

Alongside my GP and MPPI work, I am expanding into first-order policy gradients and optimization through differentiable dynamics or simulators. I am interested in contact-rich manipulation, the effects of model mismatch on gradient fidelity, robust policy updates, and connections to model-based reinforcement learning.
